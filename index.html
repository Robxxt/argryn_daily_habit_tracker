Data Privacy & Network

Your data stays on your device. Standard technical data may be visible when downloading fonts or images.

How your data is handled:
  • All your data — goals, journal entries, fears, reflections, books, notes, sessions — is stored in plain text on your device only.
  • The app runs fully locally. No personal data is sent to any server controlled by the developer.
  • Do not enter sensitive information (passwords, financial data, medical records) into any field. The storage is not encrypted.
  • Anyone who gains physical access to your device, or to a backup file, may be able to read your entries.
What connects to the internet:
  • The developer does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data. The app is designed to run offline.
  • While the app runs entirely offline, standard technical data (like your IP address) may be visible to servers when downloading necessary system fonts or images.
  • These connections are a normal part of how mobile apps work and are outside the developer’s control.
  • If you are concerned about IP privacy, you can use a VPN or revoke network access for this app in your device settings.
Notification permissions:
  • This app shows a persistent notification while a tracking session is active — displaying the activity name and a live stopwatch so you can see your progress at a glance, even when the app is in the background.
  • Android requires a visible notification for any app running a foreground service. This is a platform-level requirement and ensures you are always aware when an activity is being tracked.
  • The notification content never leaves your device. No notification data is collected, stored, or transmitted to any server.
